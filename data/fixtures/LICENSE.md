# Fixture use

Project-authored synthetic fixtures in ingestion/ may be used, copied, modified and redistributed for testing and demonstrations. They are not real filings, organizations, studies or personal records. Preserve their synthetic labeling and provenance notes. No endorsement by an organization or government agency is implied.

public-context/ contains a small factual extract of U.S. Census government statistics with separate source and retrieval metadata. Original source material and any third-party source referenced by a fixture retain their own terms; this permission does not grant rights to external datasets or licensed services.
